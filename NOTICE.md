## Bundled audio samples

The `packs/mortal-kombat/` and `packs/futurama/` directories contain short audio clips from the following third-party works:

- **Mortal Kombat** — © Midway / NetherRealm Studios / Warner Bros. Games. All trademarks and copyrights belong to their respective owners.
- **Futurama** — © 20th Century Fox / Disney. All trademarks and copyrights belong to their respective owners.

These clips are included as illustrative example packs to demonstrate how the project wires audio to coding-agent lifecycle events. Each clip is short (typically 1–3 seconds), is used in a transformative manner as functional UI feedback within a developer tool, and is attributed to its original creator above.

If a rights holder objects to the inclusion of these clips, please open an issue and the affected files will be removed promptly.

If you fork this repository — especially for public redistribution — consider replacing the bundled audio with sounds you own or have a license to redistribute. The repository's scripts work identically with any pack of `.wav` files.
